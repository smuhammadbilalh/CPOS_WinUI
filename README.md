## 📖 Overview

**CPOS** is a modern point-of-sale system for retail and small businesses, designed from the ground up for Windows with **WinUI 3** and the **Windows App SDK**. It focuses on speed at the counter, clean reporting for owners, and a Fluent-design interface that feels at home on Windows 11.

## 🎯 Planned Capabilities

- 🛒 **Fast billing** — barcode-driven checkout, discounts, returns and receipt printing
- 📦 **Inventory management** — products, categories, stock levels and low-stock alerts
- 👥 **Customers & suppliers** — contacts, purchase history and credit tracking
- 📊 **Reports & dashboards** — daily sales, top products, profit and tax summaries
- 🔐 **Users & roles** — cashier / manager / admin permissions
- 💾 **Local-first data** — works offline; data stays on your machine
